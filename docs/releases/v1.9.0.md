MacroClicker v1.9.0 adds a customizable appearance and an opt-in real-click timing test while retaining the familiar layouts and existing themes.

## Highlights

- **Custom Light and Dark:** choose two colors, rotate the window-wide gradient, adjust its strength and the locally generated matte texture, or reset the appearance. Your choices are saved independently of Classic, Modern and the previous themes.
- **Real-click stability test:** choose 10–60 seconds, review the warning and explicitly start a run. A three-second countdown gives you time to move to a safe target. Stop, the enabled start/stop hotkey, F8 or closing the test window cancels it.
- **Clear timing results:** see completed cycles, late percentage, average cadence, worst observed deadline delay and end overrun, with an optional timing-only clipboard report. Warnings, interruptions, failures and insufficient samples never receive a good verdict.

## Safety and limitations

- **The stability test sends real mouse input**, including to other applications. Use a disposable, safe target. The test measures this worker's timing and reports input-dispatch failures; it cannot prove that another application received clicks or guarantee future stability.
- Timing varies with the CPU, Windows scheduling, workload, privileges and target application. The active worker busy-spins and may consume a logical CPU and additional power, even at long intervals. The GPU does not accelerate click scheduling.

## Compatibility and updating

- Windows 10 21H2 or later and Windows 11, x64.
- Existing settings remain in `%APPDATA%\op-auto-clicker\settings.conf`; saved click settings, themes and window-scaling preferences are retained. New installations still start in Classic Light.
- Update installation is always initiated by you. A recognized source checkout requires Git, a clean `main` branch and the expected GitHub origin.
- The executable is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may request confirmation on first launch.
